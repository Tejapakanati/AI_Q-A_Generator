What strategies can be implemented to significantly increase the exports of developing countries, with a focus on doubling the least developed countries' share of global exports by 2020, as outlined in Target 17.11?\nAnswer:To significantly increase the exports of developing countries, especially focusing on doubling the least developed countries' share of global exports by 2020, several strategies can be implemented:

1. **Trade Capacity Building:** Providing support for developing countries to enhance their trade capacity through training programs, infrastructure development, and technology transfer.

2. **Market Diversification:** Encouraging developing countries to diversify their export markets to reduce dependency on a single market and increase resilience to economic shocks.

3. **Trade Facilitation:** Streamlining customs procedures, reducing trade barriers, and improving logistics to make it easier for developing countries to export their goods.

4. **Access to Finance:** Ensuring that developing countries have access to affordable credit and financial services to support their export activities.

5. **Promoting Value Addition:** Encouraging developing countries to add value to their products through processing and manufacturing, which can lead to higher export earnings.

6. **Supporting Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs):** Providing assistance to SMEs in developing countries to help them enter international markets and compete effectively.

7. **Enhancing Trade Agreements:** Negotiating favorable trade agreements with other countries to improve market access for developing countries' exports.
